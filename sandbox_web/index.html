What should I test?
  1. Known tactic. Choose Fool’s Mate, White, ply 3. Analyze. Played move should be g4, with a losing mate-in-one score.
  2. Replay the consequence. Keep “Played” selected. Press Next twice: g4 → Qh4#. After g4, legal checks include Qh4#; the final board says checkmate.
  3. Compare alternatives. Select a candidate. Its own continuation should replace the board replay. Small score gaps do not prove a unique best move.
  4. Scan the game. Find moments. Expect two White decisions scanned and g4 selected. f3 may be deep-checked, but uncertain deep evidence can leave only one final moment.
  5. Change sides. Switch to Black, read the game, analyze ply 4. Played move Qh4# should have a winning mate score from Black’s perspective.
  6. Check other sources. Use Ruy Lopez for opening context. Use the queen endgame for tablebase evidence. Network failures must be shown honestly.
  7. Reject bad input. Paste 1. e5 * and read the game. Expect an illegal-move error, with no invented result.
